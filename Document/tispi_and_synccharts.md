I’ve removed the comparison between TiSpI and SyncCharts because the differences between the two models were causing confusion.

The main difference lies in the synchronization mechanism. In SyncCharts, STGs participate in common logical reactions. In TiSpI, the Pieces within each plateau share a common position.
