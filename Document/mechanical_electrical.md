# The Mechanical-Electrical Circuit of WvAnim Tree

This Time/Space architecture is not merely a logical structure —
it also describes the functional schematic of a real electrodynamic machine.

Each component of the TiSpI system finds a direct equivalent in an electromechanical circuit: rails, gears, sliding contacts, lamps, and distribution buses. 
It is therefore possible to build, on this principle, a physical machine that executes the same temporal and spatial logic as the digital system — a contact clock where motion becomes meaning.

**Time bars → rack rails**
Horizontal toothed rails along which movement is applied. Each Piece has its own rack. They are all interconnected — mechanically synchronized.

**The gear → time engine**
It meshes with the rack and drives it forward. Continuous motion or event-triggered impulse. It is what produces time.

**The time cursor** → sliding electrical contact
It follows the gear and brushes along the rack. When it meets a vertical bar — a Key — it establishes an electrical contact.

**Keys → lamps**
They light up as the cursor passes. Each lamp carries its value: face number, position, action… The lighting is the active state.
Vertical bars → distribution bus
They connect the lamps across tracks — ensuring vertical synchronization between all pistes.
